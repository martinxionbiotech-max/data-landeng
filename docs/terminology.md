# Terminology Database

215 Chinese-English incense terms with pinyin, literal meaning, preferred translation and context. Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 for citation and reuse.

The dataset spans ingredients, forms, manufacturing and processing terms (打粉 grinding, 筛粉 sieving, 粒径 particle size, 含水率 moisture, 粘粉 binder, 成型 forming, 干燥 drying, 和料 blending, 窨 curing), agarwood grading (沉水 sinking, 栈香 semi-sinking, 黄熟香 floating, 奇楠 color grades, 头香/本香/尾香 top/body/dry-down notes), tasting technique, incense tools, and industry/trade terminology. Chinese terms are the source of truth; English is the agreed translation.

Phase 2 additions (2026-09-22) deepen four areas: **smoke & burn** (香云 fragrant smoke cloud, 烟直 straight-rising smoke, 灰白 white-ash test, 结香 resin formation), **safety & embers** (余烬 remaining embers, 灰冷 cooled ash), **recipe & formulation** (香方 incense recipe, 香药 aromatic medicine, 修合 compounding, 分两 proportions, 加减 adjust a formula), **agarwood grading residual** (蜜香 honey-scent, 鸡骨香 chicken-bone, 青桂香 green-cassia, 马蹄香 horse-hoof, 鸡舌香 chicken-tongue/clove, 叶子香 leaf, 蓬莱香 Penglai, 煎香 half-formed, 弄水香 water-stirring, 水盘香 water-basin, 重漏 heavy-leak, 白眼香 white-eye, 龙鳞香 dragon-scale, 光香 bright, 降真香 lakawood), **regional & origin** (真腊 Chenla, 占城 Champa, 渤泥 Borneo, 三佛齐 Srivijaya, 番香 foreign incense, 海南香 Hainan, 安南香 Annam), **trade & B2B** (样品 sample, 批次 batch/lot, 原产地 country of origin), and materials (麝香檀 musk-sandalwood, 婆菜香 pocai). Classical terms cite 香乘 / 香譜 on Wikisource; trade terms cite the wholesale buying guide.

Phase 3 additions (2026-09-24) add 27 terms across six directions: **scent-appreciation vocabulary** (清 clear, 甜 sweet, 辛 pungent, 凉 cooling, 厚 full-bodied, 薄 thin/light, 醇 mellow, 雅 refined), **modern perfume structure** (前调 top note, 中调 middle/heart note, 后调 base note — the modern counterparts of the classical 头香/本香/尾香), **burn & physical properties** (发烟量 smoke output, 燃烧速率 burn rate, 断火 self-extinguishing), **modern industry terms** (香精 fragrance oil, 扩香 scent throw, 留香 longevity, 定香剂 fixative), **classical 香谱 residue** (詹糖香 Zhantang, 波律香 Polu aromatic, 迷迭香 rosemary, 都梁香 Duling aromatic), and **entity-page terms** (紫檀 red sandalwood, 樟脑 camphor, 藏香 Tibetan incense, 木香花 Banksia rose, 白檀 white sandalwood).
